# Maou Drift

Maou Drift is a modified derivative of **KurumaMod** by **Deexel**, licensed under GPL-3.0-only.
Original project: KurumaMod (Deexel). All original copyright notices and the GPL-3.0 licence (`LICENSE`) are retained.

Changes: renamed to Maou Drift (`maoudrift`), original cars removed, new cars (BMW M3 GT3, BMW M4 F82,
Toyota Supra MK IV) with labPBR textures, new racing HUD, solid collision, openable doors/hood,
dynamic headlights and underglow.

Because this is a derivative of a GPL-3.0 work, Maou Drift is also distributed under GPL-3.0-only,
and its complete source must be made available to anyone who receives the mod.
